Where this comes from

Roi Ben Yaacov builds the whole thing. The same week usually holds a data model, a screen nobody has drawn yet, an argument with the App Store and a message from a neighbour who cannot find the gate. The three apps look unrelated and are the same problem each time: a small group that already knows each other, one thing there is not enough of, and no fair way to hand it round. The answer is never more features. It is one rule everybody can see, held by the server rather than by whoever shouts first. That is why Par King prices parking at one coin for twelve hours and never moves it, why Shield gives one spot per person per day, and why Royal Cash ends a night with the shortest possible list of payments. Happy to talk about any of it.
